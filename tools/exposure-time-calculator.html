The math — step wedges: each step adds a fixed optical density, so dose scales by a constant factor per step (10^density-increment — ×1.41 for 0.15). New time = old time × factor^(target − step you read). Factor filters: correct time = test time × factor of the best area. Both assume the same emulsion, mesh, lamp and distance between rounds — change any of those and run a fresh test. Very long or very short exposures can drift from the pure math (reciprocity failure), so always confirm with one more test.
